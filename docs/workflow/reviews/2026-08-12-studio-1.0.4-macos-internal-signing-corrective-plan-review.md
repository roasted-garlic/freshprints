## Summary

The plan correctly identifies a **packaging/signature** defect (not quarantine) from owner Apple Silicon smoke and from CI `.app` evidence: `identity: null` skips `@electron/osx-sign`, leaving incomplete embedded signatures without sealed `CodeResources`. Selecting ad-hoc `identity: "-"` with `hardenedRuntime: false`, adding real `codesign --strict --deep` CI gates, upgrading electron-builder to a version that supports ad-hoc identity, and keeping a separate PR from #65 is the right corrective. Publication remains forbidden.
